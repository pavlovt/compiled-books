# Appendix C. Sources and Further Reading

This book is based on major clinical guidelines and well-known trials. Figures are rounded and simplified for general readers. Guidelines are updated regularly; ask your doctor about the latest recommendations in Bulgaria.

**Guidelines**

- American Diabetes Association. *Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026.* Diabetes Care, 2026;49(Suppl 1). Summary of revisions: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12690167
- American Diabetes Association press release on the 2026 Standards: https://diabetes.org/newsroom/press-releases/american-diabetes-association-releases-standards-care-diabetes-2026
- European Society of Cardiology / European Atherosclerosis Society. *2025 Focused Update of the 2019 Guidelines for the management of dyslipidaemias:* https://www.escardio.org/Guidelines/Clinical-Practice-Guidelines/All-ESC-Practice-Guidelines/Dyslipidaemias
- European Medicines Agency. *Xigduo: summary of product characteristics* (package leaflet and prescribing information).

**Key studies mentioned**

- DiRECT trial: Lean MEJ et al. Primary care-led weight management for remission of type 2 diabetes. *Lancet* 2018; 2-year results *Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol* 2019.
- Taylor R. The twin cycle hypothesis of type 2 diabetes. *Diabetologia* 2008, and later work by the same group.
- UK Prospective Diabetes Study (UKPDS) and its 10-year follow-up. *N Engl J Med* 2008.
- Steno-2 study. Gæde P et al. *N Engl J Med* 2003 and 2008.
- PREDIMED trial. Estruch R et al. *N Engl J Med* 2018.
- Hites RA et al. Global assessment of organic contaminants in farmed salmon. *Science* 2004.
- Mozaffarian D, Rimm EB. Fish intake, contaminants, and human health: evaluating the risks and the benefits. *JAMA* 2006.
- Bouvard V et al. Carcinogenicity of consumption of red and processed meat. *Lancet Oncol* 2015.
- Reynolds AN et al. Advice to walk after meals is more effective for lowering postprandial glycaemia in type 2 diabetes. *Diabetologia* 2016.
- Shukla AP et al. Food order has a significant impact on postprandial glucose and insulin levels. *Diabetes Care* 2015; *BMJ Open Diabetes Res Care* 2017.
- Reynolds A et al. Carbohydrate quality and human health: a series of systematic reviews and meta-analyses. *Lancet* 2019.
- Covassin N et al. Effects of experimental sleep restriction on energy intake, energy expenditure and visceral obesity. *J Am Coll Cardiol* 2022.
- DAPA-CKD trial. Heerspink HJL et al. *N Engl J Med* 2020.
- DECLARE-TIMI 58 trial. Wiviott SD et al. *N Engl J Med* 2019.
- FLOW trial (semaglutide and kidney outcomes). Perkovic V et al. *N Engl J Med* 2024.

*This book is for education only and is not medical advice. Always discuss changes to your treatment with your doctor.*
