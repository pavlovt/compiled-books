# Chapter 14. Your 12-Week Action Plan

Big changes are easier in small steps. This plan builds one habit at a time over 12 weeks. Do not try to do everything at once. Each week, add the new habit and keep the earlier ones going. If a week goes badly, repeat it rather than giving up.

## Before you start

- Measure your weight and waist and write them down.
- Gather your latest test results (Chapter 2).
- Book any missing check-ups: eyes, feet, dentist, blood and urine tests.
- Tell your doctor about your plan, especially if you intend to lose weight quickly.

## Weeks 1–4: Foundations

**Week 1 – Walk after meals.** Walk 10–15 minutes after lunch and dinner every day. Take your tablets with meals, on time.

**Week 2 – Fix your drinks.** Stop all juice and sugary drinks. Drink water, mineral water, tea or coffee without sugar. Set alcohol-free days.

**Week 3 – Build your plate.** Use the plate method for lunch and dinner. Eat vegetables and protein first, carbohydrates last.

**Week 4 – Start strength training.** Two short dumbbell or bodyweight sessions this week, one set of each exercise (Chapter 6). Check your feet every evening.

**Checkpoint at week 4:** measure weight and waist. Most people see 1–3 kg less and a slightly smaller waist. Note how your energy and sleep feel.

## Weeks 5–8: Building

**Week 5 – Breakfast and fibre.** Eat a high-fibre, high-protein breakfast: oats with nuts and berries, or eggs with vegetables and rye bread. Add legumes to at least 3 meals this week.

**Week 6 – Earlier, lighter dinner.** Finish dinner 3–4 hours before bed. Make breakfast and lunch your bigger meals.

**Week 7 – Sleep.** Fix your wake-up time. Get morning daylight. Screens off 30–60 minutes before bed. Aim for 7–8 hours.

**Week 8 – Step up exercise.** Move to 2–3 sets of each strength exercise, 3 times a week. Build up to 150 minutes a week of brisk walking or cycling.

**Checkpoint at week 8:** measure weight and waist. If you have a meter, do a 3-day blood sugar profile and compare it with earlier readings.

## Weeks 9–12: Strengthening

**Week 9 – Swap the snacks.** Replace sweets, pastries and salty snacks with nuts, yogurt, fruit or vegetables. Keep tempting foods out of the house.

**Week 10 – Stress.** Add one daily stress-relief habit: a walk outdoors, a breathing exercise, or time with friends.

**Week 11 – Intensity.** If your doctor agrees, add short higher-intensity intervals to two cardio sessions a week.

**Week 12 – Review and plan.** Measure weight and waist. Get an HbA1c test if it has been 3 months since the last one. Review the annual checklist. Decide which habits to strengthen and set your goals for the next 3 months.

## What to expect

- **First 2 weeks:** weight often falls quickly, mostly water. Blood sugar after meals usually improves within days of walking and food-order changes.
- **By 3 months:** a loss of 4–8 kg is common with steady effort, with a smaller waist and a lower HbA1c. Many people notice better energy and sleep.
- **Your medicines** may need adjusting as your numbers improve. Do not change doses yourself; tell your doctor about your progress.

## When things go wrong

- **A bad day or week is normal.** Restart with the next meal, not next Monday.
- **Plateaus are normal.** Weight loss slows after a few months. Keep the habits; check portions and activity.
- **Illness or stress:** focus on the basics: medicines, fluids, walking, sleep.
- **Ask for help** from your doctor, a dietitian or family.

## The ten most important things

If you remember only one page of this book, remember this one:

1.  **Lose 5–15% of your weight** if you carry excess weight, especially around the waist.
2.  **Do not smoke.**
3.  **Keep blood pressure below 130/80** and LDL cholesterol at your target, usually with medicine.
4.  **Keep HbA1c at your target,** usually below 7%, without frequent lows.
5.  **Walk after meals,** move every day, and do strength training 2–3 times a week.
6.  **Eat a Mediterranean-style diet:** vegetables, legumes, whole grains, fish, nuts and olive oil; cut sugary drinks, juice, pastries and white bread.
7.  **Sleep 7–8 hours,** and treat sleep apnoea if you have it.
8.  **Take your medicines as prescribed,** know your sick-day rules and the warning signs of ketoacidosis.
9.  **Get your annual checks:** eyes, kidneys (blood and urine), feet, teeth, cholesterol.
10. **Look at your feet every day.**
