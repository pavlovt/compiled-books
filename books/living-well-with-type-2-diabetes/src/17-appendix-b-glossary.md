# Appendix B. Glossary

**Albumin:** a protein. When it appears in urine, it is an early sign of kidney damage.

**Beta cells:** cells in the pancreas that make insulin.

**CGM (continuous glucose monitor):** a small sensor that measures glucose every few minutes.

**DKA (diabetic ketoacidosis):** a dangerous build-up of ketones that makes the blood acidic.

**eGFR:** estimated glomerular filtration rate, a measure of kidney function.

**FIB-4:** a score calculated from blood tests that estimates the risk of liver scarring.

**GLP-1:** a gut hormone that boosts insulin after meals and reduces appetite. GLP-1 receptor agonists are medicines that copy it.

**HbA1c:** a blood test that reflects average blood sugar over 2–3 months.

**Hypoglycaemia:** low blood sugar, below 3.9 mmol/L (70 mg/dL).

**Hyperglycaemia:** high blood sugar.

**Insulin resistance:** reduced response of the body’s cells to insulin.

**LDL cholesterol:** the “bad” cholesterol that builds up in artery walls.

**MASLD:** metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, the new name for fatty liver disease linked to metabolic problems.

**Neuropathy:** nerve damage.

**Nephropathy:** kidney damage.

**Remission:** HbA1c below 6.5% for at least 3 months without glucose-lowering medicine.

**Retinopathy:** damage to the blood vessels of the retina.

**SGLT2 inhibitor:** a medicine, such as dapagliflozin, that makes the kidneys pass glucose into the urine.

**Statin:** a medicine that lowers LDL cholesterol.

**Time in range:** the percentage of time glucose stays between 3.9 and 10.0 mmol/L on a CGM.

**Visceral fat:** fat stored inside the abdomen, around the organs.
