# Appendix A. Quick Reference

## Blood sugar conversion

| mmol/L | mg/dL |
|--------|-------|
| 3.0    | 54    |
| 3.9    | 70    |
| 5.0    | 90    |
| 7.2    | 130   |
| 10.0   | 180   |
| 13.9   | 250   |
| 16.7   | 300   |

Multiply mmol/L by 18 to get mg/dL. Divide mg/dL by 18 to get mmol/L.

## Emergency signs: call 112

- Chest pain or pressure, pain spreading to arm, jaw or back.
- Face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty.
- Vomiting with stomach pain, deep or fast breathing, or confusion (possible ketoacidosis).
- Low blood sugar that does not respond to treatment, or a person who is unconscious.
- A hot, red, swollen foot with fever.

## Sick-day checklist

- Drink a glass of fluid every hour.
- Eat small amounts of carbohydrate if you cannot eat normally.
- Check blood sugar more often.
- Pause medicines according to your doctor’s sick-day plan (often metformin and SGLT2 inhibitors).
- Restart when you have been eating and drinking normally for 24–48 hours.
- Seek help if you cannot keep fluids down or feel very unwell.

## Daily foot check

- Look at both feet, including between toes and soles.
- Check for cuts, blisters, redness, swelling or colour changes.
- Wash, dry carefully, moisturise (not between toes).
- Check shoes before putting them on.
